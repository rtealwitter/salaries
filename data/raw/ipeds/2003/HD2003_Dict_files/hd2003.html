Based on 2-year classification system outlined in NCES report A Classification System for 2-year Postsecondary Institutions. The calculations are based on data from the IPEDS Institutional Characteristics, Enrollment and Completions surveys. Public 2-year small enrollment are those where sector = 4 and total 12-month unduplicated headcount is less than 2,000. Public 2-year medium enrollment institutions are those where sector = 4 and total 12-month unduplicated headcount is between 2,000 and 9,999. Public 2-year high enrollment institutions are those where sector = 4 and total 12-month unduplicated headcount >= 10,000. Not-for-profit 2-year allied health institutions are those where sector = 5 and they grant 100% of their awards in the area of allied health (CIP code = 51). Other not-for-profit 2-year institutions are those where sector = 5 and they grant less than 100% of their awards in the study year in the allied health fields. For-profit 2-year degree-granting institutions are those where sector = 6 and they grant less than 100% of their awards as certificates in the study year. Other for-profit 2-year institutions are those where sector = 6 and they grant 100% of their awards in the study year as certificates. Values = -4 if this classification is not in the study universe and -5 if the institution cannot be classified due to missing data. Classification titles differ from those in the original study, although the definitions remain the same.
